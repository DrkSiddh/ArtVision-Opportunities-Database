# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment Script: Miami Art Week Exhibition Opportunities
## Casual Coffee Chat Style – Encouraging, Uplifting, Authoritative

**Host (as if over coffee):** Hey, friend. Pull up a chair. The café’s buzzing, the espresso’s perfect, and I’ve been digging through the internet, X, Instagram—all the usual spots—for real chances to get your digital or physical art exhibited during Miami Art Week. Not the ones that just take your money and leave you hanging. We’re talking clear deadlines, real exhibition, entry to the event energy, and mostly free or with a damn good reason if there’s a fee. This is for our ArtVision special segment. Voice is always lifting you up. No gatekeeping, no punching down. Just: here’s what’s open, here’s how to step in.

Miami Art Week is late November into early December 2026—Art Basel Miami Beach public days December 4-6, with the whole city alive from about November 30. Your work can be part of that conversation.

Let’s start with the strongest free one I found.

### Opportunity 1: Render Network / Render Foundation – Miami After Dark
**Deadline:** Sources conflict a bit—some listings say October 30, 2026, others November 2 or even November 15. A September report from the Foundation mentioned selections already underway, so **check the Typeform right now** before you invest time. As of our scrape, it was still listed as open in places.

**What they’re looking for:** Digital artists. Original work themed “Miami After Dark.” Approximately 2-minute looping animations that must be rendered on the Render Network (they provide credits for selected artists so the tech barrier is covered). Select 10-15 artists.

**When and where exhibited:** December 2, 2026, at The National Hotel, Miami Beach. Your piece projected as part of a continuous 20-30 minute show on a massive 50-foot (15-meter) wall. Architectural scale. During the heart of Art Week. Full credit. Public-facing exhibition energy.

**URL for details:** https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY  
Or search “Render Network Miami After Dark open call.”

**Application snapshot:** About 9 questions on the Typeform (takes ~7 minutes). Expect name, email, short bio or statement, links to portfolio/website/Instagram, description of the proposed piece, and probably a link or sample of the work. Receipts: portfolio links + description of the animation. No fee. Web3-adjacent—decentralized GPU rendering network, so crypto/tech crowd will be paying attention.

**Why it matters:** Free entry to a high-visibility outdoor/architectural projection during the biggest art week in the US. Credits cover the render. You’re not paying to play; you’re contributing to a curated digital installation that reaches the international audience already in Miami for Art Basel.

If the form is still open, do it today. If selections are closed, keep the name for next year—this group does real activations.

### Opportunity 2: Art Miami Magazine – Featured Artist Interview (Ongoing)
**Deadline:** Ongoing / rolling. No hard cutoff. Reviewed continuously.

**What they’re looking for:** Visual artists of any medium—painting, digital, sculpture, photography, installation, etc. Emerging to established. Any nationality. Work that contributes to the contemporary scene in Miami and beyond.

**When and how “exhibited”:** Featured online interview on ArtMiamiMagazine.com, highlighted on their social channels, and considered for future special print editions distributed during Art Week and other key Miami events. Editorial visibility, not a wall in a gallery, but real reach into the collectors and professionals circulating that week.

**URL:** https://artmiamimagazine.com/open-call-for-artist-interviews/  
Email submissions to the address listed on the page (subject something like “Artist Interview Submission – [Your Name]”).

**Application snapshot:** Send 6 high-res photos of your work (with title, medium, size, year for each), an artist statement (roughly 200-400 words), and your bio / website / Instagram. That’s the main receipts: 6 images + statement + contact links. Free. No fee.

**Why it matters:** Zero cost, low barrier, and it puts your name and work in front of people who are already in Miami for the fairs. Editorial features travel further than a single booth sometimes.

### Opportunity 3: Nova Gallery – Miami Art Week 2026 Online Exhibition via Artsy (Fee, but with clear upside)
**Deadline:** November 20, 2026.

**What they’re looking for:** Contemporary practices across painting, sculpture, photography, digital, new media, mixed media, etc. Worldwide artists. Selected works presented online through Artsy during Art Basel Miami Beach.

**When exhibited:** December 3, 2026 – January 5, 2027. Online, timed to the fair week and extending after.

**URL:** https://www.artconnect.com/opportunity/gOFrOmD67OnaUQ_VOTjrH or check Nova Gallery’s site / Artsy.

**Fee:** $40 USD application fee. **Reason it’s worth considering:** Presentation on Artsy (major collector platform) timed to the most important week of the year, exposure to Nova’s collectors and advisory clients, 70% artist share of net sales, and selected artists may be considered for 12-month non-exclusive representation. Prestigious online platform + potential longer-term relationship. Not a vanity fee—actual sales infrastructure and visibility during Art Week.

**Application snapshot:** Form with up to 5 images of work, artist statement, and standard contact/bio info. Receipts: 5 portfolio images + statement. About the usual handful of questions.

**Encouragement:** If your work is ready for collectors and you can afford the modest fee, this is one of the cleaner “fee with real upside” options. Otherwise, stick to the free ones first.

### How to use this going forward
We’re building a running database. Search “Miami” plus keyword (digital, projection, free, web3, etc.) and the URL. Deadlines will be marked so expired ones drop off. New ones get added daily so you can grab 1-3 fresh opportunities each morning.

These are the ones with clear deadlines, real exhibition or visibility during Miami Art Week, and either free or a fee that buys you something substantial (platform, sales potential, representation consideration).

Go make something. Or submit what you already have. The week is coming. Your work belongs in the conversation.
